# The Opus 5.5 Inflection

This folder is separate from the rest of the repository on purpose.

The documents at the repository root are a prediction of how the market would be forced to respond
to Mythos: a change in what attackers can do. They were written between May and August 2026 and are
left as they were, so that they can be judged against what happened.

This folder is about a second, later inflection: Opus 5.5 (released September 22, 2026), a change in
what it costs to produce software. It is a different cause with different consequences, and it should
be read and judged on its own.

## Contents

- [`working-prediction.md`](working-prediction.md) — the working prediction as of November 16, 2026:
  what the cost change is, how the market responds, and what software delivery looks like as a result.

## How the two relate

They are independent arguments that happen to push in the same direction. The Mythos series says the
cost of lacking rigor went up. This folder says the cost of producing quality went down. Neither
depends on the other being right.

Where this work bears on a specific claim in the Mythos series, `working-prediction.md` lists it
under "Points of contact". Nothing in the root documents has been edited to match.

## Status

Working material. The prediction is meant to be acted on and revised as more software gets built,
not defended. Each revision should be dated.
